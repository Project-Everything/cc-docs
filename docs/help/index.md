# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

Welcome to Creative Central! View our rules and guidelines [here](../rules/index.md).

CC is a small Minecraft community server with the goal of creating a safe and fun space for all people to enjoy. We are
consistently expanding and improving the server with new experiences and features. Currently, we offer three game modes
(Plots, Earth, and Islands).

Join our official Discord server [here](https://discord.gg/Hpg5K7wgUb).

## How to Play

Join our server using the IP address `play.creative-central.net`.

You can access each of our different game modes by
right-clicking on their respective NPCs in our Hub server, or by using the direct command, i.e., `/plots`, `/earth`,
etc.

## General Commands

Shown below are some common commands used on our servers.

- `/message <player> <message>` or `/msg <player> <message>` : Send a private message to another player
- `/reply` or `/r` : Send a private message to the last person you messaged
- `/global` : Access our global chat channel
- `/list` : See which players are online
- `/seen <player>` : See when a player was last online
- `/sit` : Sit anywhere
- `/meow` : Meow to earn coins
- `/vote` : Vote for our server to earn rewards

## Voting

**Why should you vote?**

Voting for our server daily unlocks rewards and helps our server grow. Click [here](../vote/index.md) to vote for our
server.

Occasionally, we will host official voting contests on our Discord server!

## Coins

CC Coins are the cosmetic currency of the server. They can only be obtained through voting and in-game activities. Coins
are used to purchase cosmetics and do not benefit the player.

## Ranks

Creative Central is a strictly non pay-to-win or microtransaction server. We offer Sponsor ranks for those who
wish to support us and receive exclusive cosmetic perks! Contributing financially is not required, but it helps us
maintain and improve the server.

View our purchasable ranks [here](../rank/index.md).
