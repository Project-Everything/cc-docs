# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

CC Plots is our official Creative Plots server. Claim an empty 512x512 area and start creating anything from small
builds to massive structures!

- 512x512 plots
- Free build tools such as WorldEdit and goPaint
- Roleplay features (/nickname, and more coming soon!)

## How to Play

Get started on the Plots server by creating a plot using `/plot auto` or `/plot claim` on an empty plot. A plot allows
players to build and create anything they desire.

## Useful Commands

### General Commands

- `/spawn` : Teleport to the server spawn
- `//wand` : Receive a WorldEdit wand
- `/headdatabase | /hdb` : View a list of available player heads for building
- `/co inspect` : Enable/disable inspection mode, which allows you to see who modified blocks on your plot
    - Left-click or right-click to inspect blocks

### Plot Commands

- `/plot home` : Teleports you to your plot's home location
- `/plot home <number>` : Teleports you to the home location of your xth plot
- `/plot visit <player>` : Teleports you to the plot of another player
- `/plot visit <player> <number>` : Teleports you to the xth plot of another player
- `/plot add <player>` : Adds a player to your plot, allowing them to build on your plot while you are online
- `/plot remove <player>` : Removes a player from your plot
- `/plot trust <player>` : Trusts a player on your plot, allowing them to build on your plot while you are offline and
  use WorldEdit on your plot
- `/plot kick <player>` : Kicks a player from your plot
- `/plot deny <player>` : Denies a player from your plot, preventing them from entering your plot
- `/plot clear` : Clears your plot
- `/plot delete` : Deletes your plot
- `/plot info` : Displays information about your plot
- `/plot music` : Plays music on your plot
- `/plot set biome` : Sets the biome of your plot
- `/plot flag set` : Sets a custom flag on your plot.
    - Use `/plot flag list` to see a list of available flags

Sponsor and Sponsor+ players can use `/plot merge` to merge multiple plots into one.
