# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

CC Islands is our official take on the popular OneBlock game mode. Claim your island and progress through the game block
by block, exploring various biomes and unlocking new items as you go.

- Claim a OneBlock island
- EXP-based economy (Earn money by grinding mobs and mining ore)
- Compete with others in the PVP Arena

## How to Play

Get started on CC Islands by claiming an island using `/ob`. Your island can progress between phases by breaking blocks.
Each phase has a unique biome, mobs, and available blocks. As you progress through the game, you'll unlock new items and
discover hidden features.

## Useful Commands

### General Commands

- `/spawn` : Teleport to the server spawn

### Island Commands

- `/ob` : Teleport to your island
- `/ob info` : View information about your island
- `/ob reset` : Reset your island completely
    - This will delete all progress and start you over from the beginning
- `/ob settings` : View and modify your island settings
- `/ob phases` : View a list of available phases and their details
- `/ob expel <player>` : Kicks a player from your island
- `/ob ban <player>` : Bans a player from your island
- `/ob help` : View a list of available commands for your island
