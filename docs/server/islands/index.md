# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

CC Islands is our official take on the popular OneBlock game mode. Claim your island and progress through the game
block by block, exploring various biomes and unlocking new items as you go.

- Claim a OneBlock island
- EXP-based economy (Earn money by grinding mobs and mining ore)
- Compete with others in the PVP Arena
