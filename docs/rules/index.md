# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

By playing our server you agree to have read the following rules and guidelines.

## Global Rules

![Rules Banner](../public/banner/rules.png)

#### 1. Follow Minecraft/Discord TOS

You must follow the terms of service of the platform you are playing on.

- [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms)
- [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula)

#### 2. Be Respectful

No hate speech, discrimination, homophobia, racism, harassment, or doxing.
Excessively trolling and rage-baiting is not allowed.
Keep debates civil; staff will shut them down if they get too heated.

#### 3. Keep it SFW

Absolutely no NSFW content, messages, or inappropriate creations.

#### 4. English Only

You may only speak English in public chats so our team can properly moderate.

> Examples:
> - Public Chats: Discord, Global, Local, Nation Chat, Town Chat, Plot Chat
> - Private Chats: Party chat, Private messages

#### 5. No Advertising

Do not advertise other Minecraft servers, post outside links, or self-promote without staff approval.

#### 6. Chat Etiquette

Keep posts in their relevant channels.
Do not spam or flood the chat, and only ping staff for important inquiries.

#### 7. Staff Discretion & Appeals

Staff have the final say in maintaining a healthy environment.
Do not search for loopholes.
If you feel a punishment was unjust, submit a ticket in our Discord in the #tickets channel.

## Minecraft Rules

![MC Rules Banner](../public/banner/mc-rules.png)

#### 8. No Griefing

Griefing is a bannable offense with no warning.

#### 9. External Mods

Only harmless client-side mods used for quality of life (Xaero's Minimap, Optifine, Sodium, etc.) or building (eg.,
Litematica, Litematica Printer, etc.) are allowed.

#### 10. Hacked Clients

Hacked clients or client-side mods that give you a gameplay advantage are forbidden.

#### 11. No Server Lagging

Attempting to intentionally lag or crash the server will result in a permanent ban.

#### 12. IRL Trading

Trading anything on Creative Central (such as items) in exchange for anything outside of Creative Central (real-life
money, currencies on other games, etc.) is not allowed.
This includes selling art or services for real money using our platforms.

#### 13. Evasion

If you are muted or banned, you are not allowed to use an alternative account to bypass the punishment.

#### 14. Custom Items

Items designed to harm players or the server (e.g., Kill Potions, Crash Items, nuisance items) are prohibited and will
result in a permanent ban.

#### 15. PVP & Trapping

PVP is only allowed in designated zones.
Killing players in neutral areas via traps (lava, spleefing, etc.) is not allowed.

> Designated zones on each server:
> - Earth: The End, The Arena, PVP-enabled towns, Ruined towns, and Arena plots
> - Islands: The Arena

#### 16. Farms & Exploits

Exploiting bugs, item duplication, and gold farms are strictly prohibited.
While passive automatic farms are allowed, AFK farming that requires player interaction (such as using auto clickers) is
not allowed.
This includes automated fishing and grinding PVE mcMMO abilities.
Using programs such as Baritone to automate gameplay is not allowed.

## Earth Rules

![Earth Rules Banner](../public/banner/earth-rules.png)

#### 17. Towns & Claims

Town claims must not obstruct other towns from expanding.
Disconnected claims must be regional (within 5,000 blocks of the town). Ocean claims are allowed for realistic purposes,
such as creating oil rigs, ships, or real life locations.
Claim exemptions can be granted by Moderators or above.

#### 18. Naming Restrictions

Town and Nation names must follow all of our existing rules and be geographically accurate/inspired by real-life.
Name exemptions can be granted by Moderators or above.

#### 19. Respect the World

Do not destroy public utilities such as highways or paths.
Creating massive void chunks or building "cobble monsters" is forbidden.
Ruined towns cannot be intentionally destroyed.
Public farms constructed outside of Town claims can be manually removed.

#### 20. Map Art

Map art must be built in Antarctica away from existing towns.
It must adhere to our server rules.
Griefing map art is a bannable offense.

#### 21. Alt Accounts

You may have unlimited alt accounts, but a maximum of 2 accounts can participate in Towny (active resident of a Town).
Only one account may own a Town or Nation.
